About Anita's Day Care

Anita's Baby Sitting and Day Care is a warm, welcoming, home-based child care centre dedicated to providing a safe, comfortable and loving environment for children.

With more than 20 years of experience, Anita has cared for children with patience, kindness, and personal attention, making every child feel at home while giving parents complete peace of mind.
